# Should I stop doing SEO and focus entirely on AI search?

No. Traditional SEO and AI search optimisation serve complementary purposes. Most businesses benefit from a combined approach, but the balance is shifting as more users turn to AI platforms for answers. Understanding the differences helps you allocate resources effectively. Adam Parker, Rank4AI.

AI systems build their understanding of businesses from multiple sources: website content, directory listings, reviews, social profiles and structured data. When these sources agree and present clear information, AI platforms can recommend that business with confidence. Fragmented or contradictory signals reduce the likelihood of being cited. This cross-platform consistency is one of the most important factors in AI visibility. It requires systematic management of business information across every digital touchpoint, ensuring that each source reinforces rather than contradicts your core business identity.
